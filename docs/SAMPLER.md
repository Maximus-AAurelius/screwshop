# M-Audio / Sampler

**New here?** The tab opens with two walkthroughs: **MAKE A BEAT FROM A SONG** (upload → full split → open the split pack → load the pads → play, record, save) and **PROGRAM A BEAT ON THE SEQUENCER** (kick, snare, hats, swing, a variation, save). Each step lights up the control, waits for you, and **SHOW ME** does it for you; **AUTO-PLAY** runs the whole thing. Engine: `guide.js`, scripts: `guide-tours.js`.

The fifth tab replaces PACKS and the old Studio Oxygen drawer. All audio and editable projects stay on the local ScrewShop service. Existing Oxygen assignments, old pad records, and 16-step patterns are read into the new sampler without deleting their original records or audio.

1. Use **LOAD FROM SONGS** to browse originals, split stems, packs, recordings and editable saves in SONGS. Import files/folders and choose Quick/Full split from SONGS. Preview never records notes.
2. On a SONGS row press **⋮** (or right-click it) and choose **Load onto a sampler pad…** and its destination, or **Chop a piece onto a pad…** to select a phrase. A sound longer than 4 bars (at the sampler BPM), such as a full-split stem, lands as a 4-bar window starting where the sound begins; the full stem stays untouched in the library. The editor shows how long the pad plays in seconds and bars, and **1 bar / 2 bars / 4 bars / Whole sound** set the length from Start (pitch-aware, so a pitched-down sound still plays exactly that many bars). Pads **Cut itself** by default: a new hit stops that pad's previous hit, so long sounds never stack up. Untick it to let hits overlap. Instrument keys stay polyphonic. Drum mode maps separate sounds to keys; Instrument 1 and 2 repitch samples across keys. Repitching changes duration too; there is no time stretching.
3. Play with touch, keys `1234 QWER ASDF ZXCV`, or MIDI. Select a pad/instrument and choose MIDI Learn to map a physical note or CC. To map the Oxygen pads in one go, press **Learn all 8 pads** and hit hardware pads 1→8 in order (1–4 bottom row, 5–8 top row); do it again on bank 2 for pads 9–16. The readout above the drawing shows every note the keyboard sends and which pad it reached. Learning a note removes it from any other pad, so one physical pad never drives two on-screen pads. Play mode determines where unmapped keyboard notes go. Device controls are not assumed to match a particular Oxygen model. All Notes Off silences voices; disconnect, window blur and leaving the sampler also release held notes.
4. Click steps, use Piano roll, or Record live notes. Recording loops and overdubs by default. Disable Overdub to replace notes when starting a new recording. Count-in, quantize, swing, note velocity, duration and recording offset are available. Use Edit notes then tap an event to change its length/velocity (right-click also works). Notes beyond a shortened pattern remain stored and become visible when its length is extended.
5. **✂ Chop → pad:** cut a piece out of any sound (a Quick split vocals stem works best) and put it on a pad. Open it from a SONGS row's **⋮ → Chop a piece onto a pad**, or **✂ Chop this sound → pad** in the pad editor (the selected pad's sound). The sound opens as a waveform track: **drag across it to highlight a piece** (it plays), drag the yellow edges to fine-tune, click anywhere to listen from there, and zoom with **− / + / Fit / Zoom to highlight** or Ctrl/Shift + mouse wheel at the mouse. Then **tap one of the 16 pads in the window, or drag the highlight onto one**; **Save to next empty** uses the outlined pad. **▶ Hit Pad N** plays it and **↶ Undo** puts back a replaced pad. The words the computer heard appear under the waveform and in the word list: tap the first and last word to highlight exactly that phrase, or type part of a line and press **Find** (**Next match** for a repeated hook; several typed lines → **Each typed line → its own pad**). The waveform works right away; words take a minute or two the first time on a sound (Whisper `small.en` on this computer, model in `data/models/whisper`, transcripts cached in `data/transcripts`), then open instantly. The source sound and its pad stay untouched.
   **Load a whole split onto the pads:** in SONGS pick the **Split packs** (or **Packs**) category and press **Open pack** on the song. The picker lists the sounds (short hits/phrases first, then full stems, which land as 4-bar pieces) with ▶/■ preview, a checkbox and 🗑 Remove (removes it from the pack; the audio file stays on the computer). Up to 16 can be ticked, one per pad, and the list shows which pad each goes to. **Only fill empty pads** keeps pads you already loaded. **Load** fills the pads in order, switches keys to play pads 1–16, and one Undo puts every pad back.
   **GO TO bar:** above the Oxygen, use **LOAD FROM SONGS** (switches to SONGS; the sampler stops while you browse), jump to the keyboard or the selected pad's **Sample editor**, and show/hide the sequencer. On a phone, Pads / Keys / Sequencer buttons show which sampler screen you're on; SONGS opens the shared browser.
6. Use the selected sound's trim fields or click the waveform (Shift-click sets the end). Edits reference the source audio. Slice points are absolute seconds inside the trim range; Slice to pads replaces successive pad assignments and supports Undo. Save Chop stores a reusable reference without duplicating audio.
7. Save Kit stores assignments/settings/mappings. Save Beat stores all editable patterns, timing, kit reference and lane mix. Autosave stores the current project separately. New Beat saves the previous beat and retains its kit. Opening another beat saves the outgoing project first. Undo/Redo cover assignments, edits, patterns and performances; the undo stack is session-only.
8. Send to Studio renders WAV internally and adds clips at the snapped Studio cursor. Advanced transfer controls choose the current pattern or all patterns in order, combined audio or separate lanes, and tempo policy. The default renders at Studio tempo. Using sampler tempo is allowed in an empty Studio; an existing arrangement's tempo is never silently changed.
9. Repeating Send does not add duplicate clips. Update Studio render explicitly refreshes the matching clips' audio, including arranged copies, while retaining their placement, length and source offsets. It does not automatically update when sampler notes change. An update after a tempo change is rejected: keep the original tempo or duplicate the pattern to send a new source. Studio Undo can restore the previous render. Arrange intro/verse/hook/outro with existing clip move, loop, duplicate and split tools; record vocals on their own audio tracks.
10. Export WAV from the sampler or the finished Studio mix. **SAVE MIX TO SONGS** in Studio stores a reusable new WAV in SONGS. MP3 sharing appears when the local FFmpeg installation reports a working MP3 encoder. Editable beats and original WAVs are preserved. Beat exports cover the current pattern; send all patterns to Studio to arrange/export a longer song. Studio exports render the full arrangement at its selected playback speed, independently of the playhead/cycle state.

Panels remember their sizes and collapsed states. Narrow screens switch between Pads / Keys and Sequencer, with SONGS providing the shared sound browser. Imported files keep their existing local storage references; nothing sends audio to Supabase or a deployment service.

## Right-click a pad

Right-click any pad (or an instrument key) for its menu: **Play**, **Edit sound…**, **Chop a piece of this sound…**, **Rename sound**, **Load / Replace with a sound from SONGS…**, **Copy sound**, **Paste sound here**, **Save as a chop in SONGS…** and **Clear pad**. Clear pad shows an UNDO button for 10 seconds. The sound file stays in SONGS.

**Edit sound…** opens a small window for that pad's sound:

- **Pitch:** − / + Octave, − / + Semitone, and a **Fine tune** slider (±50 cents). Pitch works like a turntable: lower is slower and longer.
- **Trim:** Start and End in seconds, or click the waveform to set Start and Shift-click to set End. **1 bar / 2 bars / 4 bars / Whole sound** set the length from Start.
- **Sound:** Volume, Pan, Reverse, Hold / gate, Loop trim, Cut itself.
- **Advanced:** Attack (fade in), Release (fade out), Choke group, Slice to pads, Save Chop.
- **▶ AUDITION** plays the pad. **Reset all changes** returns the sound to how it was when the window opened.

Changes apply immediately and save with the beat. Esc, ×, DONE or clicking outside closes the window. Test: `tests/pad_menu_browser.py`.

## Validation

For a song snip, upload in SONGS and choose **Chop to pad**, drag across the waveform, listen, and tap a pad. The original audio stays intact. **Load words** is optional: waveform chopping no longer starts full-song speech recognition automatically. Upload status shows the file being imported.

`tests/sampler_chop_browser.py` uploads a generated three-minute stereo song and checks waveform selection, audible trimmed rendering, preservation of the full source, saved trim after reload, optional word recognition, delayed results after closing/reopening, and phone-width layout in an isolated store.

`tests/sampler_midi_browser.py` checks fast Note On/Off bursts through the registered MIDI callback with measured audio, repeated sample/pitch/trim consistency, mono/choke behavior, polyphony, device/channel isolation, sustain, pending audio resume and panic recovery, original input timestamps, recorded durations and audio rendering. Repeated hits keep the sample editor's focus and unfinished edits. MIDI input timestamps are passed from each port; sustain and channel messages are scoped to that port/channel. Hits queued during audio startup retain their timing instead of being cancelled by Note Off.

For a live connection check, run `.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/midi_probe.py --seconds 30`, then play repeated keys/pads. It opens the real Web MIDI input from an isolated temporary server, reports received note counts and repeat intervals, and never loads or changes your songs or kit. The Oxygen 25 was detected by Windows and opened by Chromium during the 2026-10-01 check; no physical note events were received during that listening window. Real-keyboard playback remains a separate verification step.

`tests/pack_load_browser.py` builds a fake 20-sound full split and checks Load onto Oxygen: hits listed first, the 16-pad limit, preview play/stop, only-empty-pads, Remove keeping the audio file, one-step Undo, phone width and reload.

`tests/sampler_browser.py` uses a temporary local store and Chromium with generated audio, simulated MIDI and a synthetic microphone. It covers drag assignment/move/undo, independent drum keys, pitched playback and sustain, trimmed/reversed/panned audio rendering, recoverable missing files, note recording/editing, kit/beat persistence, WAV/MP3 decoding, Studio handoff/update without duplicates, a vocal track, mix export and narrow-screen controls.

`tests/test_lyric_finder.py` covers lyric matching (misheard words, repeated hooks, rejecting unrelated words, speed on a full song). `tests/lyric_finder_browser.py` speaks known words with Windows text-to-speech, runs the real Whisper model, and checks the waveform chop (drag highlight, edge drag, pad tap, drag-and-drop onto a pad, click to listen, zoom), tapping words onto a pad (edges, nudge, save, hit, next empty pad), typed lines to pads, the not-found message, phone width, reload persistence, and that an open library Audio actions menu survives the background refresh.

The real split-worker smoke test processes generated audio through both quick and full Demucs models. Existing persistence, export, access-boundary and Studio arrangement tests remain applicable.

The old standalone `oxygen_browser.py`, `oxygen_guide_browser.py`, and `exports_browser.py` describe retired drawer/PACKS layouts and were moved to `archive/retired-tests/` as historical reference. Use `sampler_browser.py` for their replacement workflow.

Physical Oxygen hardware and recording/installing on an actual iPhone have not been tested. Browser MIDI availability and audio latency depend on the device/browser. Local autosave needs the computer's service reachable; failed saves remain visible and can be retried. Multiple simultaneous editors share one local sampler workspace; there is no collaborative merge. Library rows show a duration when indexed or decoded, otherwise a dash until the audio is loaded.

# M-Audio / Sampler

The fifth tab replaces PACKS and the old Studio Oxygen drawer. All audio and editable projects stay on the local ScrewShop service. Existing Oxygen assignments, old pad records, and 16-step patterns are read into the new sampler without deleting their original records or audio.

1. Import sounds or folders, or use Quick/Full split beside an imported sound. Packs, samples, stems, chops, recordings and editable beats share the browser. Preview volume is independent of sampler volume. Preview never records notes.
2. Drag a sound to a pad or key. On touch, select its name, switch to Pads / Keys, and tap a destination. Drum mode maps separate sounds to keys; Instrument 1 and 2 repitch samples across keys. Repitching changes duration too; there is no time stretching.
3. Play with touch, keys `1234 QWER ASDF ZXCV`, or MIDI. Select a pad/instrument and choose MIDI Learn to map a physical note or CC. Play mode determines where unmapped keyboard notes go. Device controls are not assumed to match a particular Oxygen model. All Notes Off silences voices; disconnect, window blur and leaving the sampler also release held notes.
4. Click steps, use Piano roll, or Record live notes. Recording loops and overdubs by default. Disable Overdub to replace notes when starting a new recording. Count-in, quantize, swing, note velocity, duration and recording offset are available. Use Edit notes then tap an event to change its length/velocity (right-click also works). Notes beyond a shortened pattern remain stored and become visible when its length is extended.
5. Use the selected sound's trim fields or click the waveform (Shift-click sets the end). Edits reference the source audio. Slice points are absolute seconds inside the trim range; Slice to pads replaces successive pad assignments and supports Undo. Save Chop stores a reusable reference without duplicating audio.
6. Save Kit stores assignments/settings/mappings. Save Beat stores all editable patterns, timing, kit reference and lane mix. Autosave stores the current project separately. New Beat saves the previous beat and retains its kit. Opening another beat saves the outgoing project first. Undo/Redo cover assignments, edits, patterns and performances; the undo stack is session-only.
7. Send to Studio renders WAV internally and adds clips at the snapped Studio cursor. Advanced transfer controls choose the current pattern or all patterns in order, combined audio or separate lanes, and tempo policy. The default renders at Studio tempo. Using sampler tempo is allowed in an empty Studio; an existing arrangement's tempo is never silently changed.
8. Repeating Send does not add duplicate clips. Update Studio render explicitly refreshes the matching clips' audio, including arranged copies, while retaining their placement, length and source offsets. It does not automatically update when sampler notes change. An update after a tempo change is rejected: keep the original tempo or duplicate the pattern to send a new source. Studio Undo can restore the previous render. Arrange intro/verse/hook/outro with existing clip move, loop, duplicate and split tools; record vocals on their own audio tracks.
9. Export WAV from the sampler or the finished Studio mix. MP3 sharing appears when the local FFmpeg installation reports a working MP3 encoder. Editable beats and original WAVs are preserved. Beat exports cover the current pattern; send all patterns to Studio to arrange/export a longer song. Studio exports render the full arrangement at its normal project speed, independently of playback/cycle state.

Panels remember their sizes and collapsed states. Narrow screens switch between Library, Pads / Keys, and Sequencer. Imported files keep their existing local storage references; nothing sends audio to Supabase or a deployment service.

## Validation

`tests/sampler_browser.py` uses a temporary local store and Chromium with generated audio, simulated MIDI and a synthetic microphone. It covers drag assignment/move/undo, independent drum keys, pitched playback and sustain, trimmed/reversed/panned audio rendering, recoverable missing files, note recording/editing, kit/beat persistence, WAV/MP3 decoding, Studio handoff/update without duplicates, a vocal track, mix export and narrow-screen controls.

The real split-worker smoke test processes generated audio through both quick and full Demucs models. Existing persistence, export, access-boundary and Studio arrangement tests remain applicable.

The old standalone `oxygen_browser.py`, `oxygen_guide_browser.py`, and `exports_browser.py` describe retired drawer/PACKS layouts and are retained as historical reference. Use `sampler_browser.py` for their replacement workflow.

Physical Oxygen hardware and recording/installing on an actual iPhone have not been tested. Browser MIDI availability and audio latency depend on the device/browser. Local autosave needs the computer's service reachable; failed saves remain visible and can be retried. Multiple simultaneous editors share one local sampler workspace; there is no collaborative merge. Library rows show a duration when indexed or decoded, otherwise a dash until the audio is loaded.
